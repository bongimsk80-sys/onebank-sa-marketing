One approval policy across all your banks

OneView is a control layer on top of your existing bank accounts. Your banks keep your money and execute payments; OneView gives you one view and one set of rules.

How bank connections work

An authorised user chooses Connect a bank, confirms they may share read-only data for 90 days, and authorises access at their bank through Stitch. OneView receives balances and transactions, not your login details.

You can see each connection's status and last update, refresh it, or revoke consent at any time. Revoking stops all syncing; past transactions stay in your audit trail.

Who it is for

Finance teams of two to twenty people at companies that bank with more than one institution: CFOs, financial controllers, treasury and accounts-payable staff.

Single-bank companies use OneView for the approval workflow and audit trail alone.
