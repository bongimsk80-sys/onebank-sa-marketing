Privacy notice

Last updated 2026-10-29. This notice covers this website. The OneView application has its own privacy notice, shown at sign-up.

Who we are

OneView (company registration in progress) is the responsible party for personal information collected on this website. Information Officer: privacy@oneview.co.za.

What we collect and why

When you request a demo we collect your name, work email, company and, if you choose, your phone number, role, number of banks and message. We use them to respond to your request and, only if you tick the box, to send product updates. We also record the IP address of the request to prevent abuse.

This website sets no tracking or advertising cookies.

Where it is held

Demo requests are stored in the OneView application database (Neon PostgreSQL, European Union), hosted on Vercel. These operators are bound by agreements that give protection substantially similar to POPIA, as section 72 requires. The website itself is hosted by Netlify. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

How long we keep it

Demo requests are kept for 24 months after our last contact with you, then deleted, unless you become a customer.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to its use, or withdraw consent for updates, by emailing privacy@oneview.co.za. You may also complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.
