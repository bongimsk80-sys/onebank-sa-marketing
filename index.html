Several banks means several sets of rules

Most mid-sized companies run accounts at two to five banks. Each portal has its own login, its own approval settings and its own reports.

The cash position is a spreadsheet

Someone logs into each portal every morning and adds up the balances by hand.

Approvals depend on the bank

A payment that needs two signatures at one bank may need one at another, and sign-offs happen in email or WhatsApp.

The audit trail is scattered

When the auditors ask who approved a payment, the answer is split across portals, inboxes and chat threads.

How OneView works

  1. Connect your banks

    An authorised person links each account with read-only consent. OneView never sees your banking passwords.

  2. Set your approval policy

    Choose who can initiate, review and approve, with amount bands and transfer limits per role.

  3. Approve in one place

    Every payment, from every account, follows the same policy and needs an authenticator code to approve.

  4. Keep the record

    Each step is written to a tamper-evident audit log you can export for your auditors.
