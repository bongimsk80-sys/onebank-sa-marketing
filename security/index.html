Security and data protection

What we do to protect your financial data today, and what is still on the way. We would rather be precise than impressive.

Controls in place

AreaWhat we do
Sign-inPasswords hashed with bcrypt; authenticator-app codes required for admins, approvers and executors; sessions invalidated after a password reset; rate limits on sign-in.
Separation of companiesEvery request is checked against your organisation; users without an organisation cannot read any data.
ApprovalsInitiators cannot approve their own payments; limits and roles are enforced on the server, not only in the screen.
Bank accessRead-only, consent-based access through Stitch. Bank access tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. We never ask for or store online-banking passwords.
Audit trailAppend-only and hash-chained per organisation; admins can check that it has not been altered.
TransportHTTPS everywhere with HSTS; database connections require TLS.

Where your data is held

The application runs on Vercel and its database is a managed PostgreSQL service (Neon) in the European Union. Transfers outside South Africa rely on binding operator agreements under section 72 of POPIA. We plan to move the database to a South African region before onboarding live customer bank data.

Bank data retrieved through Stitch is processed by Stitch in South Africa.

Still to come

  • Independent penetration test before the first live-data pilot
  • South African database region
  • ISO 27001 readiness programme

Report a vulnerability

Email security@oneview.co.za. We acknowledge reports within two working days and will not take action against good-faith research that avoids customer data and service disruption.
