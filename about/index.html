About OneView

We are building the control layer South African finance teams need when their money sits at more than one bank.

Bonginkosi Masuku, Founder and CEO

Bonginkosi has spent more than 16 years in enterprise risk, governance and business continuity, working alongside CFOs and audit committees at large organisations in South Africa and abroad.

He kept seeing the same problem: treasury teams juggling several bank portals, approvals that depended on the person rather than the policy, and no single record an auditor could rely on. OneView is his answer to it.
